# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General readers visiting a personal blog or portfolio. They want to quickly
understand what has been published, begin with the newest writing, and browse
backward without learning a complicated interface.

## Product Purpose

Present personal writing in a clear, approachable archive. Success means readers
can immediately recognize the publishing order, open an article with minimal
friction, and understand where they are in the chronology.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, calm, and considered. The site should feel personal and quietly
confident, with plain language and purposeful visual hierarchy.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy magazine layouts, card-heavy blog grids, decorative timelines that
compete with the writing, and generic technology-product styling. Do not rely on
animation or color alone to explain chronology.

## Design Principles

1. Make chronology self-evident.
2. Let writing remain the primary content.
3. Use restraint to improve orientation, not to erase hierarchy.
4. Keep navigation and interaction direct.
5. Preserve clarity across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible link states, semantic
dates and headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, and layouts that remain
clear when text is enlarged or the viewport is narrow.
