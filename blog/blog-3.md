# Blog 3

<p class="article-date">Published June 9, 2026</p>

A single image can hold more than one story. The same frame may feel calm,
curious, or full of possibility depending on when we return to it.

![Window example]({{ '/assets/images/window-example.jpg' | relative_url }})

That is part of the pleasure of revisiting familiar things: they stay the same,
but we bring a different point of view each time.
